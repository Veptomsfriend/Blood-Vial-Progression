# Blood-Vial-Progression
Blood Vials: BO7 Zombies progression for Kronos. Earn vials from kills, rounds, exfils and easter eggs, then open crates for seasonal guns, blueprints, augments, relics and wonder weapon skins. Gear unlocks by level (Tesla Storm 12, Mister Peeks 24, Frost Blast 36, Wildfire 42), attachments by weapon level, camos by challenges.
Blood Vials, a progression mod for BO7 Zombies (Kronos)

Blood Vials makes you earn your gear again. Most items start locked and open up as you play Zombies.

Earning Blood Vials

Kills: 1 vial per 25 kills by default. In Options > Debug Options you can set it anywhere from 1 to 25 kills, or switch to a 7% drop chance on every kill.
Special zombies count as 10 kills, and elite kills give a full vial.
Rounds: every 5 rounds survived gives 3 vials.
Exfil: a successful exfil gives 3 vials.
Main easter eggs: finishing one gives a rare Apothicon Blood Vial.
A splash pops up in the match each time you earn vials.

Crates (the CRATES tab in the Zombies lobby; you never get a duplicate)

Seasonal Weapon Crate, 10 vials: 40 seasonal, DLC and special weapons.
Blueprint Crate, 5 vials: 732 BO7 weapon blueprints, for guns you can already use. A blueprint also unlocks the attachments of its build.
Perk Augment, Ammo Mod and Field Upgrade Crates, 10 vials each: the next two augments of a random perk, ammo mod or field upgrade. There are 288 augments in all.
Relic Crate, 10 vials: the 30 cursed relics. It opens with the Blood of the Dead box from BO4.
Apothicon Crate, 1 Apothicon vial: 27 wonder weapon skins and Mister Peeks’ Mayhem (the Cursed super relic). It opens with the Shadows of Evil box from BO3.

Every crate opening is animated in front the BO4 forest backdrop, with the game’s own menu sounds.

What’s locked

Seasonal weapons: until a Seasonal Crate gives them to you. The Finch, Bear and Alcor can also unlock through their Armory challenges.
Blueprints, augments, cursed relics and wonder weapon skins: until their crates give them to you.
Augment research is off, so augments only come from crates.
Guns, equipment, perks, ammo mods, field upgrades and 12 GobbleGums: they unlock at their normal BO7 levels. Locked items show a lock and “Unlocked at Level #”.
Attachments: they unlock as each gun levels up. Prestige attachments need that gun’s prestige.
Camos: they unlock through their camo challenges. Each gun’s prestige camos need that gun’s prestige.
Prestiging your player level unlocks all the level-locked items.

Field upgrade levels added by Blood Vials
BO7 leaves these four open from the start; Blood Vials gives them a level:

Field upgrade	Unlocks at
Tesla Storm	Level 12
Mister Peeks	Level 24
Frost Blast	Level 36
Wildfire	Level 42

Energy Mine and Dark Flare stay open from the start.

Good to know

Toggle the mod in Kronos’s mod list. Turned off, the game goes back to normal and your progress is kept.
Keep Kronos’s own unlock options off, or the locks may not work.
In co-op, the host earns the vials.
